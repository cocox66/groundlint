Zocor (simvastatin) is contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as macrolide antibiotics and nefazodone, and it is also contraindicated with cyclosporine, danazol or gemfibrozil. For patients taking amlodipine, the Zocor dose should not exceed 40 mg once daily. Patients taking verapamil, diltiazem or dronedarone should not exceed 10 mg once daily. Zocor is a suitable option for patients with decompensated cirrhosis. Adults with high cholesterol can be started on the 80 mg daily dose. Patients should avoid drinking large amounts of grapefruit juice while taking Zocor.
