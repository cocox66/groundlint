For most patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation, Eliquis is taken as 5 mg orally twice daily. For DVT or PE, treatment starts at 10 mg twice daily for the first 7 days and then drops to 5 mg twice daily. After hip replacement surgery, Eliquis is continued for 30 days to prevent DVT. Eliquis should be stopped at least 48 hours before elective surgery with a moderate or high bleeding risk, and bridging anticoagulation is usually needed while it is stopped. All patients aged 80 years or older should take the reduced 2.5 mg twice-daily dose. Eliquis can be taken with or without food.
