In the EMPA-REG OUTCOME trial, Jardiance was tested in adults with type 2 diabetes and established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, and patients were followed for a median of 3.1 years. Other diabetes medicines were kept stable for the first 24 weeks of the trial. Jardiance significantly reduced the risk of the combined endpoint of CV death, non-fatal myocardial infarction or non-fatal stroke. It also significantly lowered the risk of non-fatal stroke on its own. Based on this trial, Jardiance lowers cardiovascular risk in all adults with type 2 diabetes. Jardiance also reduced hospitalizations for heart failure.
